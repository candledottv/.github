# Security

Found a vulnerability in Candle? Tell us privately.

## Report it

Email **security@candle.tv**. Don't open a public issue. If funds are at risk, put **CRITICAL** in the subject line.

Include:

- the product and its version (for the CLI, `candle --version`);
- your platform;
- the steps that reproduce it;
- what you expected to happen.

Reports are handled under Candle's [Vulnerability Disclosure Policy](https://docs.candle.tv/rights-and-reporting/vulnerability-disclosure). It covers how to report, what Candle commits to in return, safe harbor for good-faith research, and the rules: stop at proof, use your own accounts and test funds, and give 90 days before you disclose.

## Never send a secret

Don't put a private key, seed phrase, API key or token in a report, an email or an issue. If you think one has leaked, rotate it first.

## Scope

The policy covers Candle's contracts, API and apps. A repository with its own `SECURITY.md` adds the scope for that product.
